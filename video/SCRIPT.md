# Pingo voice script (90-second Athena tour)

Record each line as its own file, named `01.wav` (or `.m4a`/`.mp3`) to `10.wav`, and put them in `video/recordings/`.
One long recording with a clear pause between lines also works.

Voice: bright, warm, smiling and energetic, a little higher than your normal voice but natural, never squeaky.
Stay within each line's maximum length, or the line will run into the next scene.
